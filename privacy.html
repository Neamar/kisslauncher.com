Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 13, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the KISS Launcher app for Android, developed by Matthieu Bacconnier.

Your data is your data.

KISS Launcher does not, and will never, have access to the Internet. The app cannot transmit any data whatsoever.

Data Collection

KISS Launcher does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app runs entirely on your device and has no capability to communicate over the Internet.

Permissions

KISS Launcher requests the following Android permissions solely to provide its core functionality:

  • Contacts — Used to let you search through your contacts directly from the launcher. Contact data is read on-device only; nothing is stored or sent anywhere.
  • Phone / Call — Used to let you place calls to your contacts. KISS will never initiate a call unless you explicitly ask it to.
  • Phone State (incoming calls) — Used to automatically populate your call history within the launcher. This feature is optional and disabled by default.

Third-Party Services

KISS Launcher does not use any third-party services, analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools.

Data Sharing

We do not share any user data with any third parties, as no user data is collected in the first place.

Data Retention and Deletion

KISS Launcher does not retain any personal data. All app preferences and history are stored locally on your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Children's Privacy

KISS Launcher does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is safe for use by all age groups.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Open Source

KISS Launcher is open-source. You can review the full source code at github.com/Neamar/KISS, including the permissions manifest, to verify these claims for yourself.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us at contact@kisslauncher.com.
